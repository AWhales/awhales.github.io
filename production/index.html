Day of the Planet of the Night is a tiny mystic RPG about a night that will not stay ordinary.

You have just moved in with Uncle Frank. It is night. The house shakes. There are flashing lights at the town hall. Police will not let anyone through. The neighbour Julia is peculiar and tired. There are rats in the bins. You and a handful of other kids end up in a locked municipal basement: steam, combination locks, holes in the floor, something moving out there.

What it is

A tiny-pixel top-down RPG. Walk a night-time town and its rotting civic guts. Talk to people. Push crates. Crack combination locks. Trail a party of kids. Drop into turn-based mystic combat with timed hits.

Not swords-and-orcs. Not cozy. Not horror jump-scares. A girl, a night town, a locked basement full of steam and rats, and a cosmos that asks your star sign.
